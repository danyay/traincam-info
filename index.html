Choo Traincam Publisher

Private automation for Dan’s Traincam (@SeoulTrainCam), a railway camera in Seoul, South Korea.

The channel owner uses this tooling to authorize YouTube access and prepare the channel’s daily livestreams. Planned broadcasting tools will manage scheduled live events, descriptions and thumbnails, and send the camera’s video with its graphics to YouTube.

This app is for the channel owner’s personal use. It is not a public sign-in service and does not offer access to other people’s YouTube accounts.
